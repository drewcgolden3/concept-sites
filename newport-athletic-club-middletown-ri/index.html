What we would fix first

A consolidated home for classes, training, and membership

Right now anyone searching for the club finds only a Facebook page plus scattered Yelp, Nextdoor, and WellnessLiving listings — with no single place to see the class schedule, training options, or how to join.

  • Build one central website consolidating the class schedule, training options, and membership pricing in a single place.
  • Add online class sign-up and personal training booking instead of relying on phone calls or third-party listing sites.
  • Add a free day-pass offer with a simple form, so first-time visitors convert instead of bouncing to a competitor.

About the club

Newport Athletic Club.

Newport Athletic Club has long served Aquidneck Island as a neighborhood fitness club, drawing members from Newport, Middletown, and Portsmouth.

With a full equipment floor, group class schedule, and coaching staff, the club has built a loyal membership base even without a dedicated web presence — a real opportunity waiting to be captured.
