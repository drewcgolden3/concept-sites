What we would fix first

Give the studio a real home online, not just directory listings

Right now anyone searching for High Street Yoga lands on a patchwork of directory pages and Facebook — there's no single place to see the actual schedule, meet the teachers, or book a class.

  • One dedicated site with a live, bookable class schedule instead of scattered directory listings.
  • Teacher bios and studio photos so new students know what to expect before walking in.
  • A clear first-class offer front and center to convert curious searchers into students.

About the studio

High Street Yoga.

High Street Yoga has quietly become one of Westerly's most-loved studios, tucked into the heart of downtown where regulars stop in before work or wind down in the evening.

The studio's small class sizes and hands-on teachers make it a favorite for both lifelong practitioners and people trying yoga for the very first time.
