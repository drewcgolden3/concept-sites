About the club

Agawam Hunt.

Founded in the early 1900s in the Rumford section of East Providence, Agawam Hunt has quietly remained one of Rhode Island's most established family clubs, built around a strong golf course and a tight-knit membership.

Tennis and swimming round out a club calendar that keeps three generations of the same families coming back through the same gates every summer.
