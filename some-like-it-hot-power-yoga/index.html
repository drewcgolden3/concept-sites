What we would fix first

Make it easy to actually reach you

There's no email address anywhere on the site, including the contact page, so every question becomes a phone call. The "Find us on Social Media!" section also has no actual links or icons attached to it, which sends new students looking for the studio's Instagram straight to a dead end.

  • A visible email address on the contact page, not just a phone number.
  • Real, working social links, not a heading with nothing underneath it.
  • Class schedule and beginner information visible without a phone call.

About the studio

Some Like It Hot Power Yoga.

Some Like It Hot Power Yoga is a hot yoga and pilates studio in Hamden, CT built around the idea that heat, breath and community change how a practice feels.

Classes run for every level, from students walking in for the first time to longtime practitioners deepening a daily habit.
