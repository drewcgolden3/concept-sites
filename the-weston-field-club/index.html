What we would fix first

Keep the camp page current

The Summer Camp page still tells parents to submit medical forms "by June 22nd" — a deadline that's now more than a month past, even though the page is live and being used for sign-ups today. It's a small detail, but it's the first thing that makes a busy parent wonder what else on the page is out of date.

  • Dates and deadlines that update automatically each season.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where parents sign up.
  • One clear way to inquire, and one to register.

About the club

The Weston Field Club.

Whatever your family is looking for — a competitive team, a casual swim, or somewhere for the kids all summer — there's a program built for it here.
