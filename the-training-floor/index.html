What we would fix first

Make the site say what the gym actually does

The homepage says, in bold, "NO DROP IN CLASSES," but the main menu still has an active "Drop In" link that leads straight to a live sign-up page for drop-in access. Anyone who reads the page and clicks the nav gets two different answers to the same question.

  • One consistent policy, stated once and matched everywhere it links.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where people decide.
  • One clear way to reach you, and one to book.
