What we would fix first

Keep the small things current

The footer copyright still reads "©2021-2024," even though the site's own page metadata shows it was last touched in January 2026. It's a small thing, but a stale copyright year is one of the fastest ways to make an active, well-loved studio look like it hasn't been updated in years.

  • An automatically updating copyright year in the footer.
  • A schedule and studio details that feel as current as the practice.
  • A simple, clean way to join a class from a phone.
