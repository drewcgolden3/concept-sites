What we would fix first

Say the same thing everywhere

Right now the site tells two different stories about the courts: the meta description that shows up in search results says "5 red clay tennis courts, 3 Har-Tru tennis courts," eight total, while the main page copy says "8 Clay Tennis Courts." A family deciding whether to join shouldn't have to guess what they'd actually be playing on.

  • Programs, hours and rates readable without an account.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where people decide.
  • One clear way to reach you, and one to book.

Copper Valley Swim and Tennis Club.
