About the club

Briarcliff Swim Club.

A summer swim team, lessons for every age, and a membership that turns the pool into the family hangout from Memorial Day to Labor Day. It's the kind of club where the same families show up year after year.

You will find us right in Avon, CT. Whatever your level, there is a way in here, whether your kid races on the team or just wants to learn to swim.
