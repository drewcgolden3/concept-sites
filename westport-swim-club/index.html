What we would fix first

Give every program the same easy way to sign up

Every other program level registers straight through the club's Parks and Rec system, but Novas and All Stars summer registration is only reachable through an obfuscated email link instead of a working sign-up form. On top of that, there is no phone number published anywhere on the site, so a parent with a quick question has nowhere to turn.

  • Programs, hours and rates readable without an account.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where people decide.
  • One clear way to reach you, and one to register.
