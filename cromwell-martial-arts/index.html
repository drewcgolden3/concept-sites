What we would fix first

Keep the contact details and history consistent

The site lists two different phone numbers for the school — 860-930-8471 next to “Call or text Sensei Frank” and (860) 632-8188 in the footer — with no explanation of which to use. The footer copyright also still reads “Copyright © 2013,” even though the school has been open since 1993 and the page references much more recent events, which makes the whole site feel out of date.

  • One clear phone number, used consistently everywhere on the site.
  • A footer copyright year that updates automatically each year.
  • A history section that accurately reflects 30-plus years in Cromwell.

About the school

Cromwell Martial Arts.

Cromwell Martial Arts has been teaching students in the Cromwell and Middletown area since 1993, led by Sensei Frank and a staff that knows every student by name.

Three decades in, the school still runs on the same belt-by-belt discipline it started with, updated with a modern, welcoming approach to teaching.
