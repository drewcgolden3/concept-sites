What we would fix first

Publish a way to actually show up

There is no phone number, no contact form and no class schedule anywhere on the homepage — right now the only way to learn how to take a class is to already have Lauren's personal email address. That's a real barrier for anyone new finding the studio.

  • A visible weekly class schedule, no login or email required.
  • A simple contact form alongside the direct email.
  • A clear first-class or drop-in booking option.

About

Evolve with Lauren Yoga Studio.

Evolve with Lauren Yoga Studio is a small, welcoming yoga and pilates studio in Hamden, CT, led by Lauren and built around a calm, personal approach to practice.

The studio's strength is its intimacy — the next step is making sure anyone in Hamden can find a class time and walk through the door without already knowing someone on the inside.
