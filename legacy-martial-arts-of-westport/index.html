What we would fix first

Make sure the name on the site matches the name on the door

The footer of the current site still carries the copyright line “Ridgefield Kempo, LLC” instead of Legacy Martial Arts of Westport, so a first-time visitor scrolling to the bottom of the page runs into a name that contradicts the one in the header. It is a small detail, but it is exactly the kind of thing that makes a new family pause before enrolling.

  • Every page, top to bottom, carries the current school name.
  • Programs and belt paths readable in under a minute.
  • One clear way to reach the school, and one to visit.

About the school

Legacy Martial Arts of Westport.

Legacy Martial Arts of Westport teaches kempo and self-defense to students of every age, with a belt path that rewards steady effort and a classroom built on respect and structure.

Located in Westport, CT, the school welcomes first-time students and experienced martial artists alike, with class times built around real family schedules.
