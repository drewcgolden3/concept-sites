The studio

Pilates that starts with your body, not a routine.

Most people arrive here because something hurts, something changed, or something they love doing has started to cost them. A back that complains after a round of golf. A shoulder that never quite came back. A body six weeks after a C‑section.

Because sessions are private, nothing is generic. Michele watches how you actually move, then builds the work around it — and adjusts it the moment it needs adjusting.

"10 times to feel, 20 times to notice, 30 times to change your body."

— Joseph Pilates

Who it's for

Most people here are working around something.

Pilates builds strength without pounding your joints, which is exactly why it suits bodies that can't just go lift heavier.

Back painThe most common reason people walk in, and the most common thing that changes.

Shoulder, knee & hip rehabilitationRebuilding strength and range after injury or surgery.

Pre & postnatalIncluding C‑section recovery and diastasis recti.

Osteoporosis & rheumatoid arthritisJoint-friendly loading and bone-supporting work.

Active agingStaying strong enough to keep walking, swimming, golfing and gardening.

AthletesTennis, golf, cycling and running — core strength, balance and flexibility.

Your instructor

Michele McCauley

Michele studied under Kathy Van Patten at the Movement Center of Boston, and trained for her 200‑hour yoga certification at the Nosara Yoga Institute in Costa Rica. She's a triathlete, a surfer, a dancer, and a culinary school graduate — which is how nutrition ended up part of the studio too.

What that adds up to in practice: someone who can look at how you move, understand why it hurts, and know which piece of apparatus will fix it.
