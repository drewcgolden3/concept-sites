What we would fix first

Put the trainers on the trainers page

The current site has a "Meet the Trainers" page in the main navigation, but click into it and there is nobody there — no names, no photos, no bios. For a business built on one-on-one trust, that is the first thing a prospective client notices, and it undercuts everything else on the site.

  • Real trainer names, photos and specialties, front and center.
  • A clear, mobile-friendly way to book a first session.
  • Programs and results laid out so a visitor knows exactly what to expect.
