What we would fix first

One address, everywhere

Right now the header lists the gym at 26 Hotchkiss St while the footer lists 175 Church St, with nothing on the page saying which one is current. Someone driving over for a first class could easily end up at the wrong building. That is an easy fix, and an important one.

  • One consistent address, confirmed on the map below.
  • Class schedule and programs visible without digging.
  • A clean, mobile-first layout for people checking the gym out for the first time.
