What we would fix first

One easy way to book, not three

Right now, booking a class means picking the right one of three separate location-specific booking flows (South Burlington, Williston, and 'The Palace') and often downloading a separate mobile app just to reserve a spot, which is a lot to ask of a first-time visitor.

  • One unified booking flow instead of three separate location-specific systems.
  • A way to reserve a class straight from the website, no app download required.
  • A clearer path for first-timers to find the right class at the right location.
