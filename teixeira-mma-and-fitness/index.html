What we would fix first

Say less, and say how to reach you

The page's meta description is stuffed with hundreds of repeated location and service keyword variations, which pushes real information further down and reads as spam rather than a straightforward introduction to the gym. Despite all that text, there's still no email address published anywhere for people to actually reach out.

  • A short, honest description of the gym, not a wall of repeated keywords.
  • A visible email address, not just a phone number to call during business hours.
  • Programs and schedule easy to find, without wading through filler copy.
