What we would fix first

Make your hours make sense

Right now the site lists general hours as Mon–Fri 10am–10pm, then separately lists Members-Only Hours as Mondays & Wednesdays 8am–10am — which reads as if the gym is open two hours before its own posted opening time, with nothing explaining how the two schedules fit together. A clean concept view of your calendar removes that confusion in one glance.

  • One combined schedule that shows member hours and general hours together, clearly labeled.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where people check hours before driving over.
  • One obvious way to see today's hours without cross-referencing two lists.

About the gym

Rock Climb Fairfield.

Rock Climb Fairfield is an indoor climbing gym in Fairfield, CT built for everyone from first-time climbers to seasoned lead climbers keeping their skills sharp between outdoor trips.

Routes get reset on a regular rotation, staff are on the floor to help new climbers get oriented, and the gym runs youth teams alongside open climbing for adults.
