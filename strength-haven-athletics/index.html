What we would fix first

Give people a way to reach you

There is no email address published anywhere on the site — the only way to reach anyone is a contact form asking for name, phone and comments, with no idea when or if you'll hear back. A visible email address, answered promptly, is often the difference between a lead and a lost one.

  • Programs, hours and rates readable without an account.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where people decide.
  • One clear way to reach you, and one to book.
