What we would fix first

Give people a way to reach you

There is no phone number or email address published anywhere on the site, which is a real problem for a membership-based club. A prospective family who wants to ask about joining has no way to actually reach anyone.

  • Programs, hours and rates readable without an account.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where people decide.
  • One clear way to reach you, and one to ask about joining.
