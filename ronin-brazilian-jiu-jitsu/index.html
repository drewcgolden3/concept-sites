About the academy

Ronin Brazilian Jiu Jitsu.

Ronin Brazilian Jiu Jitsu is a BJJ academy in New Haven, CT training students from their first day on the mats through competition-level grappling.

Classes run for kids and adults alike, with an emphasis on fundamentals, discipline and a training environment built for steady, long-term progress.
