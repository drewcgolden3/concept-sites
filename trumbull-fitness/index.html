What we would fix first

Post hours that actually agree with each other

Right now the site lists two different Sunday schedules: personal training shows 10:00am–1:00pm, while open gym shows 8:00am–12:00pm. A member checking either section has no reliable way to know when the doors are actually open.

  • One hours table, pulled from a single source everywhere it appears.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where people decide.
  • One clear way to reach you, and one to book.
