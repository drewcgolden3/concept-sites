What we would fix first

Give Court 7 one clear rule

The court policy page currently says Court 7 is reserved for coaching on weekday mornings, then a few lines later says juniors and all members can play on it "if free" during that same window. Nobody reading it can tell which rule actually governs, and that kind of confusion is exactly what a phone call to the front desk turns into.

  • Programs, hours and rates readable without an account.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where people decide.
  • One clear way to reach you, and one to book.
