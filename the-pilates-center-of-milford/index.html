What we would fix first

Make the menu actually go somewhere

Right now, clicking Workouts, Pricing, Philosophy or Contact in the main menu all lead back to the same homepage — none of them open the page they promise. For a new visitor trying to find rates or book a class, that's a dead end before they've even started.

  • Every nav link opens its own page, every time.
  • Pricing and schedule readable without a phone call.
  • One clear way to book, one clear way to ask a question.

Our philosophy
