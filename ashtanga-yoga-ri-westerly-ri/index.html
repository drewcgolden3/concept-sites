What we would fix first

Fix the broken online front door before it costs new students

The existing site can't be reliably loaded, there's no confirmed email, and the domain reads as dated — for a practice built on discipline and trust, an unreliable web presence sends the wrong first signal entirely.

  • A fast, reliable, mobile-friendly site that actually loads, replacing the current unstable domain.
  • A published email and simple contact form so prospective students have a real way to reach the studio.
  • Clear explanation of Mysore-style vs. led classes for newcomers unfamiliar with the Ashtanga method.
