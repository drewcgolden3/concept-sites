What we would fix first

Facebook is your entire website — and it's limiting growth.

Shinzo Dojo relies solely on a Facebook page for its web presence, which means no real class schedule, no booking system, and lower visibility to families searching Google directly for a dojo nearby.

  • A standalone dojo website to replace Facebook as the sole online presence, improving visibility in local search.
  • A clear, browsable class schedule and program breakdown instead of scattered Facebook posts.
  • A simple booking or inquiry form so new students can take the first step without needing a Facebook account.

About the dojo

RI Martial Arts - Shinzo Dojo.

RI Martial Arts - Shinzo Dojo is an independent martial arts school located on Mineral Spring Avenue in North Providence, training students in traditional technique and discipline.

The dojo has built a dedicated community through Facebook and word of mouth, with instructors known for their patient, structured approach to teaching martial arts fundamentals.
