About the pool

Lake Dragon Aquatics.

Lake Dragon Aquatics has spent years teaching Colchester families to feel at home in the water, with small classes and instructors who genuinely enjoy watching students improve week to week.

The program balances safety fundamentals with real stroke development, so swimmers leave not just able to swim, but confident doing it. Many families return season after season.
