What we would fix first

Give people an actual way to reach the club

The homepage mentions a documentary about the club that's “available to purchase,” but there is no link or checkout to buy it — and nowhere on the entire site is there an email, a phone number, or a way to register for a class. Interested people have no next step.

  • A working link (or embedded player) for the documentary, not just a mention of it.
  • One visible email or phone number for questions and registration.
  • A simple way to sign up for a first class online.

About

Elephant In The Room Boxing Club.

Elephant In The Room Boxing Club is a working boxing gym in New Haven, CT, built on discipline, mentorship and a real neighborhood community around the sport.

The club has a story worth telling — including a documentary made about it — and on the real site that story should come with an easy way to actually reach the gym, ask a question, and sign up.
