What we would fix first

Make the class schedule tell one story

Right now, WOH FIRE and Butts & Guts are listed as Saturday 6am classes in one part of the site, but the navigation menu lists those same classes at Tuesday 6pm and Wednesday 6pm. A member trying to plan their week off that schedule could easily show up to an empty room.

  • Programs, hours and rates readable without an account.
  • Works properly on a phone, which is where people decide.
